🎮 Controls:
• Use Arrow Keys ↑/↓ to move your paddle
• Or move your mouse vertically over the canvas
• Computer automatically controls the right paddle

📋 How to Play:

  • Click Start Game to begin playing
  • Control the left paddle with arrow keys or mouse movement
  • The computer controls the right paddle automatically
  • When the ball passes your opponent, you score a point
  • Use Pause to pause/resume the game
  • Click Reset Score to clear the scoreboard
  • The ball speeds up slightly with each paddle hit
  • Ball angle changes based on where it hits the paddle
